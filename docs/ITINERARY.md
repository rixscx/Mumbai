# Seven days in Mumbai — 10 to 16 August 2026

The second half of the TB1 deliverable: a plain-Markdown itinerary that is **useful even if no app
ever ships**. Print it, or keep it open on a phone with no signal.

Every stop below is an entry in [`../data/places.mumbai.json`](../data/places.mumbai.json), referenced
by its id. Nothing is recommended here that is not in the dataset, and nothing is in the dataset that
is not sourced — so if a claim looks thin, its entry says exactly how thin, and
[`../data/VERIFICATION.md`](../data/VERIFICATION.md) says what is still unverified overall.

Claims are labelled the way [`TRIP.md`](TRIP.md) labels them: **FACT** (off a ticket or a
corroborated source), **DERIVED** (arithmetic or scheduling on facts), **VERIFY** (matters, not yet
sourced, never asserted).

---

## The constraints that actually built this plan

This is not a wish list ordered by interest. Seven hard constraints decided it, and the ordering
falls out of them almost completely.

| Constraint | Source | What it forces |
|---|---|---|
| Arrive **Dadar 05:45, Mon 10 Aug** | FACT — IRCTC ticket | Day 1 is a Dadar/Matunga morning whether or not you planned one. Only pre-07:00 openings are usable. |
| Depart **CSMT 07:55, Mon 17 Aug** | FACT — IRCTC ticket | The 17th is not a day. **Sun 16 is the last real day and the 16th is the last night.** |
| **Both bookend days are Mondays** | DERIVED | Café Madras, Ideal Corner, Kanheri Caves and the Nehru Planetarium are all shut Monday. |
| **Bhau Daji Lad shuts Wednesday** | FACT | Wed 12 Aug cannot be the wet-afternoon museum day, which is what you would otherwise use it for. |
| **Mahim's novena is every Wednesday** | FACT | Wed 12 Aug gets an anchor instead of a hole — and it is a 40,000–50,000-person anchor. |
| **Sat 15 Aug is Independence Day *and* Pateti** | FACT | That day stays out of the Fort/Colaba core and leans on the one venue confirmed open on public holidays. |
| **Peak monsoon** | FACT | Every day needs an indoor fallback, and outdoor stops are provisional in a way indoor ones are not. |

### The good news, and the bad news

**Ganesh Chaturthi is 14 September 2026 — outside this trip entirely** (FACT). No Lalbaug crush, no
immersion-day transport shutdown. Janmashtami (4 Sept) and Dahi Handi (5 Sept) are out too. That was
the largest single risk to this plan and it has evaporated.

**Parsi New Year is inside the trip, on the last two days.** Pateti is **Sat 15 Aug** and Navroz is
**Sun 16 Aug** (FACT — Indian Parsis follow the Shahanshahi calendar, which runs ~200 days behind the
global Nowruz). Six entries in this dataset are Parsi or Irani houses.

> **Do this before you fly (VERIFY):** ring Britannia, Ideal Corner, Kyani, Koolar, Mani's and
> Yazdani and ask whether they open on Navroz. I could not source a closure policy for a single one
> of them. Evidence points to *open but very busy* — a Navroz round-up advises reserving at Ideal
> Corner — but "the guides recommend eating there on Navroz" is not "it is open", and a Parsi family
> holiday is exactly the day a family-run Parsi restaurant shuts. **The plan below deliberately puts
> every Parsi meal on 11–14 August, before Navroz, so that a closure costs you nothing.**

### The other thing to fix before you fly

**Tide times for 10–17 Aug 2026 are not in this plan because they are not sourced** (VERIFY). Marine
Drive's wave-overtopping is a high-tide phenomenon and Sewri's mudflats are tide-dependent. Pull a
real Mumbai tide table and pencil the high tides against Days 5 and 7. No plausible-looking numbers
have been written here.

## Day 1 — Monday 10 August · off the train at Dadar

The train arrives 05:45. **The 25% kaali-peeli night surcharge ends at 05:00** (FACT), so a taxi at
this hour is charged at day rates.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 05:45 | Arrive Dadar (DR) | Platform, bag, coffee decision. |
| 06:00 | `dadar-flower-market` | Runs pre-dawn and winds down early — the only morning you are guaranteed to be here in time. Exact hours **VERIFY**; lane boundaries unverified. |
| 07:00 | `asthika-samaj-matunga` | One stop south on Central, or 25 min on foot. Open from 05:00. |
| 07:30 | `ram-ashraya-matunga` | Same street. **Order idli, not dosa** — at this hour only idli, upma, tea and coffee are ready. Open Mondays. |
| — | *Drop the bag* | Not a dataset entry; accommodation is out of TB1's scope. |
| 14:00 | `bhau-daji-lad-museum` | ₹20, 10:00–17:30, **closed Wednesdays — so today is the day for it.** Short hop down the Central line to Byculla. |
| 18:00 | `shivaji-park-dadar` | Sit on the katta rather than walking laps. At its best in the evening. |

**Shut today:** `cafe-madras-matunga`, `ideal-corner-fort`, `kanheri-caves-sgnp`,
`nehru-planetarium-worli`. Café Madras is the painful one — the most recommended breakfast in
Matunga, shut on the one morning you are guaranteed to be in Matunga hungry at dawn.

**If it is pouring:** the museum absorbs an afternoon, and `koolar-and-co-matunga` is indoors nearby.

## Day 2 — Tuesday 11 August · Fort and Kala Ghoda, deliberately indoor

The most rain-proof day available. Worth swapping with Day 3 if the forecast is bad.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 07:15 | `yazdani-bakery-fort` | Opens 07:00 — one of very few things serving in Fort before 09:00. Brun is hard-crusted on purpose: dunk it. |
| 10:15 | `csmvs-kala-ghoda` | Straight up to the miniature-painting and Himalayan galleries; the ground floor takes the crowds. ₹200 reported — **confirm at the counter.** |
| 13:00 | `ideal-corner-fort` | **Tuesday is the day to come:** dhun daar patio and railway mutton are the reported Tuesday specials. Ask what is on rather than reading the board. Shut Mondays. |
| 15:00 | `david-sassoon-library-kala-ghoda` | **Hours unverified — opportunistic, not scheduled.** The balcony is members-only; the ground floor and garden are the visit. |
| 17:30 | `marine-drive-promenade` | Walk the northern end towards Marine Lines for quiet. **Stay behind the railing and off the tetrapods** — people are killed there every monsoon. |
| 19:00 | `kyani-and-co-marine-lines` | Open to ~20:00. Chai and bun maska together; mawa cake to take away. |

**If it is pouring:** this day barely changes. That is why it exists.

## Day 3 — Wednesday 12 August · the novena, then Aarey — the longest day

Bhau Daji Lad is shut today, which removes the obvious fallback. In exchange Wednesday is the only
day two of the best things in the dataset are available at all.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 06:00 | `st-michaels-church-mahim` | **Wednesday is the entire point.** Novenas roughly hourly, 06:00–21:30, in five languages; a reported 40,000–50,000 attendees across the day. **Take the 06:00 or 07:00 slot** — the middle hours are packed. Walk from Mahim Junction; the streets are impassable by car. |
| 08:30 | `aarey-picnic-point` | Mahim → Goregaon on Western, then a rickshaw in. 08:00–19:00. **August is the point here** — green, high birdlife. Keep to roads and garden: documented leopard landscape. |
| 17:30 | `ghatkopar-khau-galli` | Nothing opens before ~16:30. Goregaon → Andheri on Western, then **Metro-1 east to Ghatkopar** — that line exists to avoid going via town. Entirely vegetarian; **Jain pani puri near Guru Kripa**. |

**Honest warning:** this is the most demanding day in the plan — three corners of the city and a lot
of train time. Drop Ghatkopar without guilt if the novena and Aarey have used you up.

**If it is pouring:** the novena still runs and the church is covered, though the queue is not. Aarey
does not work in a real downpour and today's museum fallback is closed — so swap the whole day with
Day 2 and keep Fort for the rain.

## Day 4 — Thursday 13 August · Kanheri Caves

A full day and the longest journey in the plan. Closed Monday, so it has to live mid-week.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 07:30 | `kanheri-caves-sgnp` | 07:30–17:00, last entry ~16:30. Borivali on Western, rickshaw to the park gate, then ~6 km inside. **Park gate fee and the ₹15 cave ticket are separate** — carry cash; the gate figure is unsourced. |
| — | | Caves shelter you; the climb between them does not. Basalt steps in monsoon are slick and there are unfenced drops. Closed shoes with grip. |
| 19:00 | `cafe-madras-matunga` *or* `koolar-and-co-matunga` | Back via Western to Dadar/Matunga. **Café Madras is open today** — the Monday problem does not apply. |

**If it is pouring:** genuinely reconsider. Slippery rock at height in heavy rain is the one stop
where bad weather is a safety question rather than a comfort one. Fall back to
`nehru-science-centre-worli` and move Kanheri to Sunday.

## Day 5 — Friday 14 August · Girgaon, Malabar Hill, and the last Parsi lunch

Friday deliberately avoids Chor Bazaar, whose Friday hours are unresolved. It is also **the last day
before Pateti and Navroz**, which is why Britannia sits here.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 09:30 | `khotachiwadi-girgaon` | Charni Road, then a short walk. **Somebody's street, not a site** — ask before photographing houses or people. Half an hour is the whole visit. |
| 11:00 | `banganga-tank-walkeshwar` | Up the hill. The **tank** is open ground and always accessible; the **temples** keep conflicting hours and several shut early afternoon. Wet stone steps beside deep water — careful. |
| 13:00 | `britannia-and-co-fort` | **Lunch only, shuts 16:00, cash only.** Open Mon–Sat, so Friday works and Sunday never would. Arrive by 13:00 if the berry pulao matters. **Deliberately placed before Navroz.** |
| 17:00 | `marine-drive-promenade` | Second pass — worth timing to a **high tide** if your tide table says one falls this evening. |

## Day 6 — Saturday 15 August · Independence Day and Pateti, so: Bandra then Worli

Two overlapping events. Restrictions around CSMT, the Gateway, Marine Drive and government buildings
are **VERIFY** but likely, so this day stays north of them — and ends somewhere confirmed open on
public holidays.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 09:30 | `ranwar-village-bandra` | Bandra station, west via Hill Road. Walk Waroda Road and Chapel Road and the lanes between. Residential — ask before photographing. Founding date is disputed in the sources, so nobody should quote one. |
| 14:00 | `nehru-science-centre-worli` | **Open every single day, public holidays included** — the reason this day works. ₹40, 09:30–18:00, counter shuts 17:30. Bandra → Worli by rickshaw or taxi. |
| 15:00 | `nehru-planetarium-worli` | About a kilometre away, and a **separate institution with a separate ₹150 ticket.** Timed dome shows only: **15:00 is the English one.** Shut Mondays, open today. |

**If it is pouring:** ideal, almost. Skip Ranwar and give the whole day to the two Worli venues —
both fully indoor, and one of them cannot close on you.

## Day 7 — Sunday 16 August · Navroz, and the last real day

Britannia is closed Sundays regardless. Chor Bazaar's week runs Saturday to Thursday, so Sunday works
for it. **It is Navroz** — expect Parsi and Irani houses to be busy or shut (**VERIFY**), which this
day is built to not depend on.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 05:00 | `sassoon-dock-colaba` | **The one unmissable early start left.** Auction and peak 03:30–07:30; by 08:00 it is being hosed down. Shoes you can ruin. Photography restricted — naval facilities adjoin; ask, and expect no. **August caveat:** the west-coast monsoon fishing ban may thin landings badly (**VERIFY**). |
| 11:00 | `chor-bazaar-mutton-street` | Reported 11:00–19:30. Opening asks run 2.5–4× real price; close at 35–45% of first quote on antiques. Assume "old" means reproduction. Cash. |
| 15:00 | `sewri-fort` | Harbour line to Sewri, then <10 min on foot — unsignposted, so read the route in the entry. Free, unstaffed. **The flamingos are not here in August** — Nov–Mar migrants. Not ideal alone. |
| 19:00 | `five-gardens-matunga` *or* `dadar-parsi-colony-walk` | A last easy evening near Dadar before packing. The 16th is the last night. |

**If it is pouring:** drop Sewri — an exposed overgrown ruin is the worst wet-weather stop in the
dataset — and use `bhau-daji-lad-museum` or `nehru-science-centre-worli` instead.

Shut today anyway: `cafe-madras-matunga`, `ideal-corner-fort`, `kanheri-caves-sgnp`,

## What this plan is missing, stated plainly

The dataset behind it is **29 entries across 13 of 21 clusters**, and 28 of the 29 appear above. The
plan is honest but thin in places, and these are the gaps that would most improve it:

- **Navroz opening hours for six Parsi and Irani entries.** The single highest-value phone call
  available. Until it is made, Days 6 and 7 avoid Parsi food entirely.
- **Tide times**, as above. Two stops depend on them.
- **Bandra, Khar, Santacruz** — two entries across three clusters, and both are in Bandra/Mahim.
  Day 6's morning is thinner than it should be, and there is no sourced Bandra *eating* at all.
- **Andheri, Versova, Juhu, Powai, Sion-Wadala, Thane, Navi Mumbai, BKC** — nothing sourced. Eight
  clusters at zero. The Madh–Versova ferry in particular could be a genuine monsoon entry or a
  suspended service, and nobody has checked.
- **No accommodation, and no food between stops.** Deliberate — out of TB1's scope — but it means
  this is a skeleton to hang a day on, not a complete day.
- **Monday closures beyond the four now known.** Both bookend days are Mondays.
- **Whether CSMVS closes on Independence Day**, which is why it sits on Tuesday rather than Saturday.

Nothing above was padded to fill a day. Where a slot is empty it is because the dataset has nothing
sourced to put in it, and an invented recommendation would be worth less than a visible gap.
