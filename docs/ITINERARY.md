# Seven days in Mumbai — 10 to 16 August 2026

The second half of the TB1 deliverable: a plain-Markdown itinerary that is **useful even if no app
ever ships**. Print it, or keep it open on a phone with no signal.

Every stop below is an entry in [`../data/places.mumbai.json`](../data/places.mumbai.json), referenced
by its id. Nothing is recommended here that is not in the dataset, and nothing is in the dataset
that is not sourced — so if a claim looks thin, its entry says exactly how thin, and
[`../data/VERIFICATION.md`](../data/VERIFICATION.md) says what is still unverified overall.

Claims are labelled the way [`TRIP.md`](TRIP.md) labels them: **FACT** (off a ticket or a
corroborated source), **DERIVED** (arithmetic or scheduling on facts), **VERIFY** (matters, not yet
sourced, never asserted).

---

## The constraints that actually built this plan

This is not a wish list ordered by interest. Five hard constraints decided it, and the ordering falls
out of them almost completely.

| Constraint | Source | What it forces |
|---|---|---|
| Arrive **Dadar 05:45, Mon 10 Aug** | FACT — IRCTC ticket | Day 1 is a Dadar/Matunga morning whether or not you planned one. Only pre-07:00 openings are usable. |
| Depart **CSMT 07:55, Mon 17 Aug** | FACT — IRCTC ticket | The 17th is not a day. **Sun 16 is the last real day and the 16th is the last night.** |
| **Both bookend days are Mondays** | DERIVED | Café Madras, Ideal Corner and Kanheri are all shut Monday. Three of the best entries in the dataset are unavailable on two of the nine days. |
| **Bhau Daji Lad shuts Wednesday** | FACT — sourced | Wed 12 Aug cannot be the wet-afternoon museum day, which is exactly what you would otherwise use it for. |
| **Peak monsoon** | FACT | Every day needs an indoor fallback, and outdoor stops are provisional in a way indoor ones are not. |

Plus two that shape individual days:

- **Chor Bazaar's Friday is unresolved** — one source says closed for Jumma with an afternoon
  reopening, another says closed but for a dawn trade-only market. So Friday is not the day to go.
- **Sat 15 Aug is Independence Day** (FACT). Restrictions around CSMT, the Gateway, Marine Drive and
  government buildings are **VERIFY** — expected, not confirmed. The plan keeps that day out of the
  Fort/Colaba core rather than betting on it.

### The one thing to fix before you fly

**Tide times for 10–17 Aug 2026 are not in this plan because they are not sourced** (VERIFY). Two
stops depend on them — Marine Drive's wave-overtopping is a high-tide phenomenon, and Sewri's
mudflats are tide-dependent. Look up a real tide table for Mumbai before day 5 and pencil the high
tides against Days 5 and 7. No plausible-looking numbers have been written here.

## Day 1 — Monday 10 August · off the train at Dadar

The train arrives 05:45. **The 25% kaali-peeli night surcharge ends at 05:00** (FACT), so a taxi at
this hour is charged at day rates.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 05:45 | Arrive Dadar (DR) | Platform, bag, coffee decision. |
| 06:00 | `dadar-flower-market` | Runs pre-dawn and winds down early — this is the only morning of the trip you are guaranteed to be here in time. Exact hours **VERIFY**; lane boundaries unverified. |
| 07:00 | `asthika-samaj-matunga` | One stop south on Central, or 25 min on foot. Open from 05:00. |
| 07:30 | `ram-ashraya-matunga` | Same street. **Order idli, not dosa** — at this hour only idli, upma, tea and coffee are ready; specials from ~06:00, full menu ~06:30. Open Mondays. |
| — | *Drop the bag* | Not a dataset entry; accommodation is out of scope for TB1. |
| 14:00 | `bhau-daji-lad-museum` | ₹20 Indian adult, 10:00–17:30, **closed Wednesdays — so today is the day for it.** Byculla is a short hop down the Central line. |
| 18:00 | `shivaji-park-dadar` | Sit on the katta rather than walking laps. Packed and at its best in the evening. |

**Shut today, do not walk to them:** `cafe-madras-matunga` (closed Mondays — the single most
recommended breakfast in Matunga, shut on the one morning you are guaranteed to be hungry in
Matunga at dawn), `ideal-corner-fort`, `kanheri-caves-sgnp`.

**If it is pouring:** the museum absorbs a whole afternoon, and `koolar-and-co-matunga` is indoors
and nearby.

## Day 2 — Tuesday 11 August · Fort and Kala Ghoda, deliberately indoor

The most rain-proof day available, so it is worth holding for genuinely bad weather and swapping with
Day 3 if the forecast says so.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 07:15 | `yazdani-bakery-fort` | Opens 07:00 — one of very few things serving in Fort before 09:00. Brun is hard-crusted on purpose: dunk it. Closing time and any Sunday closure **unverified**. |
| 10:15 | `csmvs-kala-ghoda` | Go straight up to the miniature-painting and Himalayan galleries; the ground floor takes the crowds. Fee reported ₹200 Indian adult — **confirm at the counter**, it is aggregator-sourced. |
| 13:00 | `ideal-corner-fort` | **Tuesday is a good day to come:** the kitchen runs day-wise specials, and dhun daar patio and railway mutton are the reported Tuesday dishes. Ask what is on rather than reading the board. |
| 15:00 | `david-sassoon-library-kala-ghoda` | **Hours unverified — treat as opportunistic, not scheduled.** The reading-room balcony is members-only; the ground floor and garden are the visit. |
| 17:30 | `marine-drive-promenade` | Walk the northern end towards Marine Lines for quiet. **Stay behind the railing and off the tetrapods** — people are killed there every monsoon. |
| 19:00 | `kyani-and-co-marine-lines` | Open to ~20:00. Chai and bun maska together; mawa cake to take away. |

**If it is pouring:** this day barely changes — CSMVS and Ideal Corner are both indoors. That is why
it exists.

## Day 3 — Wednesday 12 August · Aarey, then Ghatkopar after dark

Bhau Daji Lad is shut today, which removes the obvious wet-weather fallback — so this is the day to
spend on the one entry monsoon actively *improves*.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 08:30 | `aarey-picnic-point` | 08:00–19:00. **August is the point here** — green, and high birdlife. Take a rickshaw in from Goregaon; it is well inside the colony. Keep to roads and garden: this is a documented leopard landscape. |
| 17:30 | `ghatkopar-khau-galli` | Nothing opens before ~16:30. Goregaon → Andheri on Western, then **Metro-1 across to Ghatkopar** — that line exists to avoid going via town. Entirely vegetarian; **Jain pani puri near Guru Kripa**. |

**If it is pouring:** Aarey does not work in a genuine downpour and the museum fallback is closed.
Swap this whole day with Day 2 and keep Fort for the rain.

## Day 4 — Thursday 13 August · Kanheri Caves

A full day, and the longest journey in the plan. Closed Monday, so it has to live mid-week.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 07:30 | `kanheri-caves-sgnp` | 07:30–17:00, last entry ~16:30. Borivali on Western, rickshaw to the park gate, then ~6 km inside. **Park gate fee and the ₹15 cave ticket are separate charges** — carry cash for both; the gate figure is unsourced. |
| — | | Rock-cut caves shelter you; the climb between them does not. Basalt steps in monsoon are slick and there are unfenced drops. Closed shoes with grip. |
| 19:00 | `cafe-madras-matunga` *or* `koolar-and-co-matunga` | Back via Western to Dadar/Matunga. Café Madras is open today — the Monday problem does not apply. |

**If it is pouring:** genuinely reconsider. Slippery rock at height in heavy rain is the one stop in
this plan where bad weather is a safety question rather than a comfort one. Fall back to
`bhau-daji-lad-museum` (open Thursday) and move Kanheri to Sunday.

## Day 7 — Sunday 16 August · the last real day

Britannia is closed Sunday. Chor Bazaar's week runs Saturday to Thursday, so Sunday works for it.

| Time | Stop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 05:00 | `sassoon-dock-colaba` | **The one unmissable early start left.** Auction and peak 03:30–07:30; by 08:00 it is being hosed down. Shoes you can ruin. Photography restricted — naval facilities adjoin; ask, and expect no. **August caveat:** the west-coast monsoon fishing ban may thin landings badly (**VERIFY**). |
| 11:00 | `chor-bazaar-mutton-street` | Reported 11:00–19:30. Opening asks run 2.5–4× real price; close around 35–45% of first quote on antiques. Assume "old" means reproduction. Cash. |
| 15:00 | `sewri-fort` | Harbour line to Sewri, then <10 min on foot — unsignposted, so read the route in the entry. Free, unstaffed. **The flamingos are not here in August** — they are Nov–Mar migrants. Come for the fort and the harbour view. Not ideal alone. |
| 19:00 | Pack | The 16th is the last night. |

**If it is pouring:** drop Sewri — an exposed overgrown ruin is the worst wet-weather stop in the
dataset — and use `bhau-daji-lad-museum` (open Sunday) or `csmvs-kala-ghoda` instead.

## Monday 17 August — not a day

CSMT departure 07:55 means leaving accommodation around **06:45** (DERIVED). Nothing is scheduled.
`yazdani-bakery-fort` opens at 07:00, which is *after* you need to be at the platform — noted only so
nobody talks themselves into it.

Everything shut today anyway: `cafe-madras-matunga`, `ideal-corner-fort`, `kanheri-caves-sgnp`.

## What this plan is missing, stated plainly

The dataset behind it is **26 entries across 12 of 21 clusters**. The plan is therefore honest but
thin in places, and these are the gaps that would most improve it:

- **Bandra, Khar, Santacruz** — one entry across three clusters. Day 6 suffers directly. The Mahim
  Church Wednesday novena and the Mount Mary feast are exactly the hyper-specific weekly rituals
  this dataset exists for, and neither is sourced yet.
- **Lower Parel / Worli / Prabhadevi** — nothing. The Nehru Science Centre and Planetarium are the
  obvious missing wet-afternoon options in the whole plan.
- **No accommodation, and no food between stops.** Deliberate — out of TB1's scope — but it means
  this is a skeleton to hang a day on, not a complete day.
- **Festival dates are unresolved.** Ganesh Chaturthi, Parsi New Year and Janmashtami 2026 dates are
  all unsourced. If Ganesh Chaturthi falls in this window it changes the entire trip, and the plan
  above would need rebuilding around it. **This is the highest-value unresolved question in the
  project.**
- **Tide times**, as above.
- **Monday closures beyond the three named ones** — the list is certainly incomplete, and both
  bookend days are Mondays.

Nothing above was padded to fill a day. Where a slot is empty it is because the dataset has nothing
sourced to put in it, and an invented recommendation would be worth less than a visible gap.
